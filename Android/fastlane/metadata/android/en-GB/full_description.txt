Inkwell is a native reader and writer for the Standard.site publishing ecosystem on AT Protocol.

This is an experimental Android companion to Inkwell for iOS, the primary client. It is a work in progress toward feature parity, so expect rough edges.

Read site.standard.publication and site.standard.document records directly from the author's PDS. Renders Leaflet block content, Markpub markdown, pckt/Offprint (converted to markdown with facet-aware formatting), and other formats generically so nothing is silently dropped. Resolves display themes from Leaflet's rich palette through basicTheme to Material 3 defaults. Comments, likes, reposts, and replies are shown.

Discover publications via the cross-platform public index, then fetches authoritative records from the author. Subscribe to publications and recommend documents, with live recommend counts from Constellation (microcosm.blue). Paginated feed with previous/next navigation.

Write and publish site.standard.document records with portable metadata. Content format selection is functional — choose between Leaflet, Markpub, pckt, and Offprint, each producing the correct content type. Image upload and blob handling are supported.

Verifies publications and documents against their claimed domain via Standard.site's `.well-known` and link-tag checks, shown as a badge on both feed cards and the post detail screen.

Authentication is OAuth-based with your AT Protocol handle — no app password required. Silent session restoration on relaunch.

Inkwell is free, with no ads, tracking, or in-app purchases. All code is AGPL-3.0.
